A comprehensive full-stack ERP web platform for APAMAN, centralizing administrative, financial, and medical operations. Features a Beneficiary Management System (BMS) with an integrated Electronic Health Record (EHR) subsystem, automated Financial Management (FMS), Procurement (PMS), Inventory (IMS), and Human Resources (HRIS), all secured by strict Role-Based Access Control (RBAC).
