A custom AI framework developed at CERN to orchestrate and automate the full life cycle of GAN-based Generative Models, including Data Preprocessing, Model Training, Model Evaluation, and Uncertainty Quantification. Designed to accelerate LHCb particle physics simulations, operating within a high-performance computing environment with rigorous software-engineering practices.
